# 投稿与审稿模板

以下模板只提供结构。把方括号内容替换为真实信息，提交前由作者核对。期刊有专用表格时，以期刊表格为准。

## 投稿信息采集表

```yaml
manuscript:
  title: "[论文题目]"
  article_type: "[Original Article / Review / ...]"
  field: "[研究领域]"
  abstract: "[摘要或文件路径]"
  keywords: ["[关键词1]", "[关键词2]"]
  version: "[v01]"
authors:
  corresponding_author: "[姓名]"
  affiliation: "[单位]"
  email: "[邮箱]"
  orcid: "[ORCID]"
journal:
  name: "[期刊名称]"
  guide_url: "[官网 Guide for Authors 链接]"
  checked_on: "[YYYY-MM-DD]"
submission:
  stage: "[选刊 / 投稿前 / Submitted / With Editor / Under Review / Revision]"
  manuscript_id: "[如有]"
  submitted_on: "[YYYY-MM-DD，如有]"
constraints:
  deadline: "[截止日期，如有]"
  funding: "[基金信息或无]"
  ethics: "[审批编号或不适用，必须真实]"
  data_availability: "[声明或链接]"
deliverable: "[计划 / 清单 / Cover Letter / 回复表 / 状态邮件]"
```

## Cover Letter 草稿

```text
Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled “[MANUSCRIPT TITLE]” for consideration as a [ARTICLE TYPE] in [JOURNAL NAME].

In this study, we [STATE THE RESEARCH QUESTION OR OBJECTIVE]. Using [DATA, MATERIALS, OR METHODS], we found that [STATE THE MAIN FINDING IN ONE OR TWO SENTENCES]. These findings [STATE THE SCIENTIFIC OR PRACTICAL SIGNIFICANCE] and may be of interest to readers of [JOURNAL NAME] because [EXPLAIN THE FIT WITH THE JOURNAL'S SCOPE OR RECENT WORK].

The main contributions of this work are:
1. [CONTRIBUTION OR NOVEL FINDING]
2. [CONTRIBUTION OR METHOD]
3. [IMPLICATION OR VALIDATION]

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to [JOURNAL NAME]. [STATE ANY REAL CONFLICT OF INTEREST, ETHICS APPROVAL, DATA AVAILABILITY, OR FUNDING INFORMATION REQUIRED BY THE JOURNAL.]

Thank you for considering our manuscript. We would be grateful for the opportunity to have it evaluated. Please contact [CORRESPONDING AUTHOR] at [EMAIL] if additional information is needed.

Sincerely,
[CORRESPONDING AUTHOR NAME]
[AFFILIATION]
[EMAIL]
```

Cover Letter 中的创新点、期刊适配和声明必须能在论文或真实记录中核对。不要夸大“首次”“突破”或接受概率；如果期刊要求建议审稿人或回避审稿人，按系统字段单独填写。

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 推荐/回避 |  |  |  |  | 无合作者、导师、同单位等直接冲突 |  |

只填写能够从公开机构页面或论文中核实的信息。期刊不一定采纳推荐；推荐本身不能成为交换审稿意见或影响决定的工具。

## 状态询问邮件

```text
Subject: Status inquiry for manuscript [MANUSCRIPT ID] — “[MANUSCRIPT TITLE]”

Dear Editorial Office,

I am writing to inquire politely about the status of our manuscript “[MANUSCRIPT TITLE]” (Manuscript ID: [ID]), submitted on [DATE]. The current system status is “[STATUS]”.

We understand that the review process takes time and appreciate the editors' and reviewers' efforts. Could you please let us know whether any additional information is needed from our side?

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[CORRESPONDING AUTHOR]
[AFFILIATION]
[EMAIL]
```

把询问时间建立在期刊当前公开周期和真实提交日期上；如有毕业、基金或项目截止日期，只陈述事实，不施压。

## Response to Reviewers 开头与逐条格式

```text
Dear Editor and Reviewers,

Thank you for the careful and constructive comments on our manuscript. We have revised the manuscript according to the comments and highlighted the changes in [LOCATION OR FILE]. Our point-by-point responses are provided below. Reviewer comments are reproduced in bold, followed by our responses.

### Reviewer 1, Comment 1

**Comment:** [ACCURATE QUOTE OR CONCISE PARAPHRASE]

**Response:** We agree that [ACKNOWLEDGE THE POINT]. We have [DESCRIBE THE ANALYSIS OR EDIT] and added [EVIDENCE]. The corresponding change appears on page [X], lines [Y–Z] / in Figure [N] / in Supplementary Table [N].

**Change in manuscript:** “[PASTE THE FINAL REVISED SENTENCE OR DESCRIBE THE CHANGE]”
```

如果没有采纳建议：说明具体原因、数据或方法边界，提供替代分析或在局限性中承认影响，并让编辑能够快速定位证据。回复表、修订稿和补充材料中的数字必须一致。

## 投稿前最终核对

- [ ] 期刊范围、文章类型、格式和最新截止日期已从官网核实并记录日期。
- [ ] 题目、摘要、关键词、作者顺序、单位、邮箱和 ORCID 一致。
- [ ] 正文结构、字数、引用、图表编号、分辨率和文件名符合要求。
- [ ] Cover Letter、Highlights、Graphical Abstract 和 Supplementary 文件齐全或明确不适用。
- [ ] 利益冲突、伦理、知情同意、数据、基金和作者贡献声明基于真实信息。
- [ ] 推荐或回避审稿人没有直接合作或其他利益冲突，联系方式可核实。
- [ ] 预览 PDF 已逐页检查，保留最终版本和文件清单。
- [ ] 已由作者核对并明确确认最终 Submit；提交后保存 Manuscript ID 和确认邮件。
